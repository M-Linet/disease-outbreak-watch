Articles come from Google News search results for disease names in English and Kiswahili. A TF-IDF and logistic regression model decides which ones report an outbreak, and rule-based extraction pulls out the disease, place and numbers. A pair counts as rising when it has at least two reports this week and at least twice its weekly average over the previous four weeks. These are leads to verify against official sources, not confirmed outbreaks.
